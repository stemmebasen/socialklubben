# 0006: Socialklubben: all of the cultural life, and going together

Status: Accepted (2026-10-17)

## Context

The chair renamed the project and widened it:

> "Vi skal have flere kategorier med så det dækker et bredt udsnit af aarhusiansk kulturliv"

> "Vi kommer til at omdøbe projektet Socialklubben.dk og konceptet bliver et sted hvor man kan blive en del af en cirkel og så tage til events sammen."

The proposed categories were accepted, and the region stays all of Østjylland. How circles work is undecided.

## Decision

- **The name is Socialklubben** (socialklubben.dk). The code, the Worker, the database and the app use it.
- **Thirteen categories** replace the seven themes: Musik & koncerter; Scene: teater, dans & comedy; Film; Kunst & udstillinger; Foredrag & debat; Litteratur; Mad & drikke; Krop & sind; Sport & friluft; Kreative workshops; Fællesskab; Marked & loppemarked; Fest & natteliv. The old themes live on in "Krop & sind" and "Fællesskab". A category still comes from plain words, never from AI.
- **The tone stays professional, modern and elegant.** The rule against a spiritual vibe came from the narrower portal and is dropped; the site is simply a calm guide for everyone.
- **Region:** all of Østjylland, as before.
- **Circles are not built yet.** The about page says that the part about going together is being developed. Nothing else promises it.

## Consequences

- No data had to move: the portal had not been put live.
- Curation (decision 0002) stays: organizers and finds are still approved by people.
- Once circles are decided, they get their own decision record. Open questions include how people find a circle (matched by interests and area, or made by friends), its size, and whether it builds on Cirkler.net.
