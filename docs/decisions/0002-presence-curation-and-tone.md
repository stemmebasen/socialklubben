# 0002: Curated, present in the city, and professional in tone

Status: Accepted (2026-10-02)

## Context

The chair added two requirements after the first version.

The first is about the portal's reason to exist: "Det er vigtigt at få med at portalen skal være eksklusiv og ikke kun leve online, hvor den ellers ville få svært ved at få en eksistensberettigelse. Den skal være levende til stede, have god kontakt med byens steder, som aktivt skal bruge portalen, og vi skal også være synlige i det offentlige rum, f.eks. med plakater og brochurer. Platformen i sig selv skal være simpel, men udbydere skal se platformen som nødvendig for at komme ud til publikummet med events inden for området."

The second is about the expression: "Siden må ikke have en spirituel vibe, den skal være professionel, moderne og elegant."

## Decision

- **Curated, not open listing.** Organizers are added by the administration after an assessment against explicit criteria (Blueprint, section 3). Organizers who are in are shown alike. Nobody pays for a place or a position. The public pages say so: "udvalgt efter relevans og kvalitet".
- **Presence is the product, the website is the backbone.** Partner venues, posters, brochures and a social media profile are part of the vision. The system's part is to generate printed material from the same data, with QR codes back to the matching page. This keeps the platform simple and the print always correct. The steps are in Blueprint section 7, and each is decided when the one before has use.
- **A professional tone.**
  - The cockpit's soft violet and turquoise glow and the ring mark are removed. The brand is a plain wordmark with the region in small capitals.
  - The copy leads with "Foredrag, kurser og workshops inden for mental sundhed, selvudvikling og spiritualitet".
  - Themes have plain names ("Spiritualitet", "Fællesskab", "Selvudvikling") and are shown with mental health and personal development first. The order they are matched in is unchanged.
  - Esoteric imagery is never used.

## Consequences

- The portal's own frame and language stay calm and trustworthy, whatever an organizer's event text says.
- The next build step proposed is the printable programme (a poster and a brochure per town and week or month, with QR measurement). Partner venues need a person to keep in touch with them, which is an open decision.
